Privacy Policy

This app ("Social Uploader") is a personal tool used to upload photos and videos to connected social media accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) on behalf of its owner.

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data belonging to third parties. Access tokens for connected accounts are stored securely and encrypted, and are used solely to publish content that the owner has selected.

This app does not use data for advertising, does not sell data, and does not share data with any third party outside of the platforms the user explicitly connects (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok).
